## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an aspiring **Data Analyst** with a strong software engineering foundation and a growing focus on **data-driven problem solving and business intelligence**.

I enjoy taking a problem from **raw data → analysis → visualization → business insight**.

My current analytics toolkit includes:

- **SQL / MySQL** for querying, aggregation, data exploration, and analytical problem solving
- **Python** for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and visualization
- **Pandas & NumPy** for data manipulation and numerical analysis
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** for exploratory visualization
- **Excel** for analysis, reporting, and business-focused data work
- **Power BI & DAX** for interactive dashboards, KPIs, and analytical reporting
- **Snowflake & AWS** for cloud/data-platform exposure
- **Git & GitHub** for version control and project documentation

### 🎯 What I Bring

> **Technical thinking + analytical thinking + business curiosity**

I don't want to build dashboards just to make them look good.  
I want to understand **what the data is saying, why it is happening, and what action a business should take next.**
